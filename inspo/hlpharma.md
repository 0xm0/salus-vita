## Storage & Distribution: Drug Scheduling Guide

### Hero

**Headline:**
Understanding Drug Scheduling in Australia

**Body:**
The Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) classifies medicines into schedules based on their risk level and the required degree of regulatory control.

HL Pharma holds licences to import, store, and distribute pharmaceutical medicines and medical devices under Schedules 2, 3, 4, 8, and 9.

---

### Schedule Descriptions

- **Schedule 2 (Pharmacy Medicine):** Low-risk medicines available over the counter but may require pharmacist advice.
- **Schedule 3 (Pharmacist-Only Medicine):** Requires consultation with a pharmacist but does not require a prescription.
- **Schedule 4 (Prescription-Only Medicine):** Requires a prescription from an authorised healthcare professional.
- **Schedule 5 (Caution):** Substances with low toxicity or concentration require caution in handling, storage, or use.
- **Schedule 8 (Controlled Drug):** Medicines with a high potential for abuse or dependence. Strictly regulated.
- **Schedule 9 (Prohibited Substance):** Restricted to medical or scientific research under strict controls.

**Compliance:** HL Pharma ensures all pharmaceutical medicines and medical devices are stored in accordance with manufacturer guidelines and Australian regulatory requirements.
