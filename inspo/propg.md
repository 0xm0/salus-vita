## Corporate Responsibility, Ethics and Modern Slavery Statement

### Headline

CORPORATE RESPONSIBILITY, ETHICS AND MODERN SLAVERY STATEMENT -- Pro Pharmaceuticals Group Pty Ltd

### Introduction

ProPG operates in a regulated healthcare setting and emphasizes disciplined governance, ethical conduct, patient protection, and responsible supply chain management.

### Governance and Ethical Conduct

The company maintains a formal Code of Conduct covering ethical behaviour, conflicts of interest, confidentiality, regulatory compliance, and lawful conduct.

ProPG maintains a zero-tolerance approach to bribery, corruption, facilitation payments, improper inducements, and unethical commercial practices.

ProPG operates under a pharmaceutical wholesale licence issued by the Victorian Department of Health and complies with TGA, SAS, and pharmacovigilance requirements.

### Human Rights and Modern Slavery

The organization is committed to preventing modern slavery, forced labour, human trafficking, child labour, and exploitative labour practices.

### Responsible Supply

ProPG sources medicines only from authorised, quality-assured partners operating within regulated frameworks.

### Workplace Standards

The company maintains a safe, lawful, and respectful workplace aligned with labor laws and anti-discrimination requirements.

### Data Protection

ProPG complies with the Australian Privacy Principles and applicable data protection laws.
